CHAPTER SIX

EDUCATION

From an 8th-century Buddhist university to a modern state university — Bhagalpur has been a place of learning for a very long time.

THE FLAGSHIP

Tilka Manjhi Bhagalpur University (TMBU)

Established in 1960 as Bhagalpur University and renamed in 1991 after the freedom fighter Tilka Majhi, TMBU sits on a sprawling 264-acre campus and runs eight faculties — arts, science, social science, humanities, commerce, management, law, and education — split across three dozen-plus departments. It oversees a wide network of constituent and affiliated colleges across the Bhagalpur and Banka districts.

T. N. B. College

Founded in 1883, one of the oldest degree colleges in the city, spread over a 60-acre campus with its own stadium.

Marwari College

Established in 1941, offering undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in commerce, arts, and science, plus BBA, BCA, and biotech courses.

Jawaharlal Nehru Medical College

Set up in 1971, admitting around 120 MBBS students a year and now affiliated with the Bihar University of Health Sciences.

Bhagalpur College of Engineering

Established in 1960 under the Bihar government's Department of Science and Technology, now affiliated with Bihar Engineering University.

Sericulture & agricultural research

A dedicated sericulture institute and an agricultural research station support the region's silk and farming economy with technical training.

Vikramshila (historic)

Long before any of the above, the Buddhist university at Vikramshila made this stretch of the Ganga a centre of scholarship for hundreds of years.
